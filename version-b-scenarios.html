Decision-Readiness Assessment

Scenario-Based Format

How this works: Each question presents a realistic scenario facing institutions that fund, govern, or shape creative ecosystems. Pick the response that best describes how your institution would actually behave — not how you wish it would. Each option reflects a different level of decision readiness.
Topic 1 — Visibility

Your institution is asked to present a map of the creative ecosystem it supports — capabilities, gaps, and emerging work. What happens?

Topic 2 — Coordination

A cross-sector partnership opportunity emerges that requires alignment between your institution, a local authority, and several creative organisations. How does coordination play out?

Topic 3 — Measurement

A major creative economy programme you funded has been running for two years. The board asks whether it delivered value. What can you show them?

Topic 4 — Investment Readiness

A promising creative economy proposal has been declined by your investment committee for the third time this year. What's the pattern?

Topic 5 — Foresight Capacity

An emerging shift in the creative economy — a new technology, a changing demographic, a policy development — is starting to reshape how creative organisations operate. How does your institution respond?

Please answer all 5 scenarios before submitting.
