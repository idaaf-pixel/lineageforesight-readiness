Decision-Readiness Assessment

For institutions that fund, govern, or shape creative ecosystems. Three formats, same five dimensions. Try each and compare.

Version A

Likert Scale

Rate your agreement with five statements about your institution's decision-readiness across the creative ecosystem, from "Strongly Disagree" to "Strongly Agree."

Quick to complete Familiar format Quantifiable Risk of neutral bias
Version B

Scenario-Based

Read a realistic institutional scenario and pick the response that best matches how your institution would actually behave. Each option maps to a maturity level.

Contextual Harder to game Built-in maturity model Takes longer
Version C

Binary + Branching

Start with a Yes/No question about your institution, then answer a targeted follow-up based on your response. Two-step format digs deeper into root causes.

Adaptive Reveals root causes Conversational feel More interactive

All three versions cover the same five dimensions:

  1. Visibility — Can you see what's happening across the creative ecosystem you support?
  2. Coordination — How effectively do you coordinate across partners, sectors, and governance layers?
  3. Measurement — Do you have credible methods for assessing whether your investments deliver value?
  4. Investment Readiness — Is your institution structured to evaluate and commit to creative economy opportunities?
  5. Foresight Capacity — Can you anticipate shifts in the creative economy and act ahead of them?
Purpose: This is a format comparison test for institutional decision-makers. Try all three versions and consider: Which felt most natural? Which revealed the most honest answers? Which would programme managers, foundation officers, and public authority leads actually complete without disengaging?
