Decision-Readiness Assessment

Binary + Branching Format

How this works: Answer each Yes/No question honestly. A follow-up question will appear based on your answer to dig deeper into your institution's reality.
Topic 1 — Visibility

Does your institution have a current, consolidated view of the creative ecosystem it supports?

Follow-up

How reliably does that view inform your decisions?

Follow-up

What do you rely on instead?

Topic 2 — Coordination

Does your institution have established processes for coordinating with partners across sectors and governance layers?

Follow-up

Do those processes lead to clear, shared decisions?

Follow-up

What happens when cross-sector coordination is needed?

Topic 3 — Measurement

Does your institution formally assess whether its creative economy programmes and investments deliver value?

Follow-up

Do those assessments capture outcomes and learning, or just activity?

Follow-up

Why not?

Topic 4 — Investment Readiness

When a creative economy proposal is declined, is the reasoning clearly communicated to the applicant?

Follow-up

Does that feedback help applicants strengthen future proposals?

Follow-up

What effect does that have on the organisations you support?

Topic 5 — Foresight Capacity

Has your institution adjusted a programme or investment priority in the last 12 months based on an anticipated shift in the creative economy?

Follow-up

How did the institution respond to the outcome of that adjustment?

Follow-up

What's the main barrier to acting on emerging shifts?

Please answer all primary questions and their follow-ups before submitting.
